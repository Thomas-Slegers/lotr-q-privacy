# Delete Your LOTR-Q Account

If you would like your LOTR-Q account and associated data to be deleted, follow these steps:

1. Send an email to **Thomsleg2@gmail.com** from the email address associated with your LOTR-Q account (or mention your account's display name and sign-in method — Google, Apple, or email/password).
2. Include the subject line: **"Account Deletion Request"**.
3. We will confirm your identity and process your request within a reasonable timeframe.

## What Gets Deleted

Upon request, we delete:
- Your account profile (display name, email address)
- Your gameplay data (quiz history, scores, ELO rating, streak)
- Your friend list and challenge history
- Your step-tracking data (if opted in)
- Your push notification token

## What May Be Retained

Some data may be retained for a limited additional period where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes (for example, records needed to resolve an in-progress friend challenge with another user). Any such data is not used for any other purpose and is deleted once no longer required.
