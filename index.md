# Privacy Policy for LOTR-Q

**Last updated:** September 2026

LOTR-Q ("the App") is developed by Thomas Slegers as an independent, hobbyist project. This policy explains what information the App collects, why, and how it is used.

## Information We Collect

**Account information.** When you sign in with Google or Apple Sign-In, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from that provider. This is used to create and identify your account within the App.

**Gameplay data.** We store your quiz answers, scores, streaks, ELO rating, in-app currency (Mithril) balance, and friend/challenge activity. This data is used to power the App's core features: daily quizzes, rankings, and friend challenges.

**Step count data.** If you choose to opt in to the step-tracking feature, the App reads your daily step count via Health Connect (Android) to calculate progress toward a group step goal. This data is only read with your explicit permission and only while the feature is enabled. You can disable this at any time in the App's settings.

**Push notification token.** If you allow notifications, the App stores a device token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) used solely to deliver in-app notifications, such as friend challenge invites.

## How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to operate the App's features: authentication, quiz gameplay, rankings, friend challenges, and step-goal tracking. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Where Your Data Is Stored

All data is stored using Google Firebase (Firestore, Cloud Functions, Firebase Authentication), hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure in the `europe-west1` region.

## Data Sharing

Your display name, rank, and quiz results may be visible to other users of the App (e.g., friends, challenge opponents, and shared leaderboards), as this is core to the App's social features. We do not share your personal information with third parties outside of the service providers necessary to operate the App (Google Firebase).

## Data Retention and Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email address below. We will delete your data within a reasonable timeframe, except where retention is required for legal or security purposes.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
